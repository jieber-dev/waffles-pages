🧇 Waffles

A personal Telegram assistant

Waffles is a private, personal-use Telegram bot built by Jasper Ang. It helps capture day-to-day tasks and events from natural-language messages, keep a morning briefing of the day's calendar and to-do items, and answer quick questions like "what's on today" or "what's my workout".

Waffles is not a public product and is not available for anyone else to install or use — it exists solely for its one owner/operator.

See the privacy policy for details on what data it accesses and how it's used.
